Welcome to my newish personal site.

This is a work in progress, and probably always will be. I've moved away from traditional portfolio sites to something that feels closer to how I actually work and think - like a mix between a professional archive and a creative dump. Some finished things, some half-baked, and some are just ideas written down. Or at least, that's the vibe I'm trying to go for.

You'll find things like:

- **Research & papers** - academic work, projects tied to my PhD and current research. The serious stuff. 
- **Game prototypes & experiments** - small games, strange mechanics, things I built just to see if they were possible.
- **Music & art** - sketches, sounds, visuals, assets. Most of this is under CC0, so do whatever you want with it.
- **Posts** - like this one. Thoughts, process stuff, whatever I feel like writing about. I do have a backlog of things I want to write about, so we shall see how that goes.

Everything is organised by "modes" (code, game, art, music, research, writing, (maybe more in the future)) and you can filter by whatever interests you. Most of my own work is released under CC0, because I believe digital things shouldn't be hoarded.

If you want to know how I built this site (spoiler: vibe coding turned out to be reaaally fun), I wrote about that [here](/posts/how-i-built-this-site/).

Hopefully this place keeps growing. Welcome.
