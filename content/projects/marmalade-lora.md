My first LoRA, trained on Krea 2 with 19 images of [Marmalade](/projects/marmalade/). It's absolutely amazing at putting her in all kinds of situations, styles, etc., but it sometimes gets her eyes a bit wrong or her in general, as any LoRA will.

To use it, put "Marmalade the catgirl" in your prompt. I use it on Krea 2 Turbo at 0.9 strength. The example images on the LoRA page have their ComfyUI workflow saved inside, so you can drag one into ComfyUI and it loads everything.
