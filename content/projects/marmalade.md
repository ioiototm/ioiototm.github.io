Marmalade is a CC0 orange catgirl. She isn't really mine, she's everyone's. That's basically what the name of her site means: marmalade.you, she is yours.

She started as "Gothfield", a LoRA I found while messing around with image models. It was way too close to another orange cat to release as CC0, so she got a new name, Marmalade, or Mal for short. After that I started drawing her myself, and slowly she turned into her own thing with a bit of a personality.

I release everything I make with her as CC0, source files and timelapses included, so you can see how stuff is made and build on top of it. So far that's stickers, illustrations, a pixel art bust, cup designs and the PNGTuber model I stream with. Other people have started making things with her too, which is my favourite part.

I also trained a [LoRA of her](/projects/marmalade-lora/) for Krea 2, so you can generate Mal yourself.
